## The problem

La Mafia Tumbada is a corridos tumbados and regional mexicano group from Xalapa, Veracruz, active
since 2021. Their music is on Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube; their audience is on Instagram and
TikTok.

None of those platforms is built to take a booking. Someone who wants the band at a festival, a bar
or a private party lands on a profile and has to guess who to message.

## What I built

An official site that gathers everything a promoter needs to decide and book: the catalogue of
singles, each linking straight to the platform it lives on, the videos, stage photos, who's in the
band and what the project is about. It all ends in the same place — a WhatsApp button with the
message pre-written, and the band's phone number.

No form and no server: the site is static, and the conversation happens where the manager already
replies. Built with Astro.

## Result

The band has a single link to hand out, and booking requests arrive straight on the phone instead of
as a DM buried under fan replies.
