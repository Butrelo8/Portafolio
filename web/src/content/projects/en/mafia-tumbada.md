## The problem

La Mafia Tumbada is a corridos tumbados and regional mexicano group from Xalapa, Veracruz, active
since 2021. Their music is on Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube; their audience is on Instagram and
TikTok.

None of those platforms is built to take a booking. Someone who wants the band at a festival, a bar or a private party lands
on a profile and has to guess who to message.

## What I built

An official site that does the one thing the platforms don't: takes a booking request. Visitors get
the project and every platform in one place, and a direct path to request a quote for live shows,
festivals and private events, delivered as email through Resend.

Astro for the site, Hono for the booking endpoint, with Zod validating what comes in.

## Result

The band's manager has a single link to hand out, and booking requests arrive as email instead of
as a DM that gets buried under fan replies.
