## The problem

Coast is a two-day urban dance competition and workshop series at Foro Boca, Veracruz. All of it
lived on Instagram: the instructors, the schedule, ticket prices, the venue, and every question a dancer
had before signing up.

Instagram is good at announcements and bad at reference. A dancer deciding whether to travel needs
the price tier, the class schedule and the venue in one place — not scattered across three months of
stories.

## What I built

A dedicated site covering the whole event: instructor lineup, workshops, day-by-day agenda, pricing
tiers, venue details and an FAQ, with registration as the single clear call to action. Announcements
still break on Instagram, and the site stays the reference that outlives the story.

Built with Next.js and Tailwind, deployed on Cloudflare.

## Result

The event has a permanent home that answers a dancer's questions without a DM, and a link that works
in a bio, a flyer and a WhatsApp forward.
