I'm Ivan, a web developer in Veracruz, México. I build sites and tools for businesses that already
exist: a dance competition, a car care shop running since 1982, a regional mexicano group, a marine
monitoring company.

What interests me isn't the nice-looking site, it's the problem behind it. It's usually the same
one: something that matters is being coordinated over WhatsApp, over Instagram, or in a
spreadsheet, and it has stopped holding up. That's where I'm useful.

## How I work

**I ask what's breaking first.** Before proposing any technology I want to know what eats your
time, what gets lost, and what your customers ask you ten times a day.

**I ship things that run without me.** A panel your team actually uses, bookings that arrive
without you answering a message, a record that outlives whoever stopped maintaining it.

**I speak plainly.** No jargon. If something isn't worth building, I'll say so.

## What I work with

- **Sites and platforms:** Astro, Next.js, React, TypeScript
- **Server and data:** Node, Hono, Express, PostgreSQL, Drizzle
- **Infra:** Cloudflare, Docker

I pick the tool for the problem, not the other way around.
